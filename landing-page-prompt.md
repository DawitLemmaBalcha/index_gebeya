# Landing Page — Creative Brief for AI Designer

## The TL;DR

You are designing the landing page for **Index Gebeya** — "The Search Engine for Ethiopia's Telegram Marketplace." This page lives at `indexgebeya.cfd` and is what people see when they visit from a regular browser (not inside Telegram). Use this prompt exactly as context when generating the design.

---

## The Soul of the Platform

Ethiopia has a massive, invisible economy happening on Telegram. Thousands of channels post products every day — phones, cars, laptops, shoes, apartments, livestock. But every channel is a silo. You can only see products from channels you already follow. There's no way to search across them. If you want an iPhone 13 under 50,000 birr, you have to join twenty channels and scroll for hours.

**Index Gebeya fixes this.** We monitor hundreds of Telegram channels, extract every product, classify it with AI, and make it searchable. We are not a store. We are not a marketplace. We are a **search engine** — an index, like our name says. We connect a buyer's intent directly to a seller's Telegram post.

The platform lives inside Telegram as a Mini App. When you open it from the bot, you get the full marketplace — search, browse, alerts, seller reviews. The landing page is for everyone else: someone who got a link, found us on Google, or was told about us by a friend.

---

## The Problem This Page Must Solve

Someone lands on this page. They have never heard of Index Gebeya. They may or may not use Telegram. In 5 seconds, they need to understand:

1. **What is this?** — It's a search engine for Ethiopian Telegram products.
2. **Why does this exist?** — Because Telegram channels are invisible to the world.
3. **Why should I care?** — Because the thing you want to buy is probably already posted somewhere. We'll find it.
4. **What do I do next?** — Open it in Telegram. You already have the app.

If they leave confused, the page failed.

---

## Creative Direction & Constraints

### DO NOT:
- Use generic startup landing page templates. No blue-purple gradient hero with floating abstract shapes. No cheesy stock illustrations of people pointing at floating screens.
- Use lorem ipsum or placeholder content. Every word matters.
- Make it look like a SaaS product. We are not a dashboard, a CRM, or a productivity tool.
- Use American or European cultural references. This is Ethiopian. The context is Ethiopian. The currency is birr. The channels are Ethiopian Telegram channels.
- Make it feel corporate. We are not a bank, an insurance company, or an enterprise tool.

### DO:
- Be specific. Use real product examples: "Toyota Vitz 2019 — 1.2M birr" not "cars."
- Use Amharic snippets sparingly and naturally if they fit. "ምን ትፈልጋለህ?" (What are you looking for?) could be a section header.
- Make the search bar the hero of the page. We are a search engine. The search experience should be front and center.
- Use bold typography. Simple, confident, direct. Let the words do the heavy lifting.
- Think about mobile first. Most Ethiopians access the web on phones. The page must look incredible on a 375px-wide screen.
- Use a color palette that feels Ethiopian without being a cliché — think warm earth tones, deep greens, rich yellows, not just green-yellow-red flag stripes.
- The page should feel fast and light. No heavy animations. No 5-second loading screens. Instant, like a search engine should be.

### Visual References / Vibe Direction
- Think about DuckDuckGo's cleanliness but with more personality.
- Think about how print editorial design uses typography hierarchy — big bold headlines, confident subtext, generous whitespace.
- Think about how Ethiopian coffee ceremonies feel — warm, deliberate, un-rushed, every element intentional.
- Take creative risks. If you have a wild idea that might be brilliant, try it. I will judge the output, so impress me.

## Content Structure

The page is a single scroll. No multi-page navigation. Everything the visitor needs is on one page.

### 1. Hero Section — The First Impression

**Must include:**
- Tagline: "The Search Engine for Ethiopia's Telegram Marketplace" or a creative variation that captures the same meaning
- A one-line explanation: "Thousands of products are posted on Ethiopian Telegram channels every day. We index them so you can search across all of them at once."
- A clear Call to Action: "Open in Telegram" — links to `https://t.me/IndexGebeyaBot`
- A secondary link: "Browse on Web" for those who want to peek without Telegram

**Creative opportunities:**
- An animated search bar cycling through real search examples
- A counter showing live product count
- An illustration that represents "indexing" or "connecting" without being generic

### 2. How It Works — Three Steps

| Step | Concept | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Search & Browse | Search across thousands of products from Ethiopian Telegram channels. Filter by price, category, location. |
| 2 | Set Alerts | Looking for something specific? We'll notify you on Telegram the instant a match appears. |
| 3 | Always in Telegram | No separate app. No website to bookmark. Lives where the sellers already are — inside Telegram. |

**Creative opportunities:** Numbered steps with visual distinction. Iconography that doesn't feel like generic SaaS icons.

### 3. Live Stats Bar

Three numbers pulled from the API:
- **X products** indexed
- **Y channels** monitored
- **Z categories** covering everything from phones to livestock

These are dynamic. They prove the marketplace is alive.

### 4. Category Showcase

A visual grid showing what you can find — not links, just proof of breadth:

Phones · Laptops · Vehicles · Property · Shoes · Fashion · Furniture · Jewelry · Gaming · Livestock · Agriculture · Services

Each with an icon or visual treatment that feels cohesive but not templated.

### 5. FAQ — Answer the Silent Questions

- **"Why Telegram?"** — Because that's where the sellers are. We don't ask them to move. We index their posts where they already live.
- **"Do you sell products?"** — No. We connect you directly to the seller's original Telegram post. You deal with them. We're the bridge, not the store.
- **"Is this free?"** — Completely free for buyers.
- **"I'm a seller. How do I get my products listed?"** — Add our bot to your Telegram channel as an admin. Your products will appear automatically.
- **"Is this only in Ethiopia?"** — Today, yes. The marketplace is Ethiopian, the currency is birr, the channels are Ethiopian.

### 6. Footer

Minimal. Link to the bot. Copyright. Location: Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

## What I Will Reject

- Generic AI-generated landing pages that look like every other startup's website
- Hero sections with floating geometric shapes and gradient blobs
- Text that sounds like it was written by a committee or a marketing bot
- Anything that feels like a template from a website builder
- Designs that only work on desktop and fall apart on mobile

## What I Want

A production-grade, creative, opinionated landing page that feels like it was designed by a human who understood the soul of the product. Take risks. Be specific. Make it memorable.
